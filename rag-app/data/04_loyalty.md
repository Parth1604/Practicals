# Cloud Club Loyalty Program

Cloud Club is Nimbus Coffee's free loyalty program, available through the Nimbus mobile app.

## Earning points
Members earn 1 "drop" for every $1 spent. Bringing a reusable cup earns 5 bonus drops per visit.

## Rewards
- 50 drops: free pastry
- 100 drops: free drink of any size
- 250 drops: 340 g (12 oz) bag of whole bean coffee

## Tiers
Members who earn 1,000 drops in a calendar year reach "Storm" tier. Storm members get free milk alternatives, a free birthday drink, and 10% off all retail coffee beans for the following year.

Drops expire 12 months after they are earned. Drops cannot be exchanged for cash.
