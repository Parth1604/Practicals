# Current Coffee Lineup

## Stratus Blend (house espresso)
Brazil Cerrado and Colombia Huila. Medium roast. Tasting notes: milk chocolate, toasted almond, caramel. $17 per 340 g bag.

## Altocumulus — Ethiopia Yirgacheffe
Washed process, grown at 1,900–2,200 meters by the Konga cooperative. Light roast. Tasting notes: jasmine, lemon zest, bergamot. $21 per 340 g bag.

## Thunderhead — Sumatra
Wet-hulled process from the Gayo highlands of Aceh. Dark roast. Tasting notes: dark cocoa, cedar, molasses. $18 per 340 g bag.

## Drizzle Decaf — Colombia
Decaffeinated with the sugarcane (ethyl acetate) process, which removes about 97% of caffeine. Medium roast. Tasting notes: brown sugar, red apple. $18 per 340 g bag.
