# About Nimbus Coffee Co.

Nimbus Coffee Co. was founded in 2014 by siblings Lena and Marco Albrecht in Portland, Oregon. The first shop opened on Alder Street in a converted bicycle repair garage. The company name comes from Lena's childhood habit of naming clouds.

Nimbus now operates 12 cafés across Oregon and Washington and roasts all of its coffee at its roastery in Hillsboro, Oregon. The roastery uses a 15-kilogram Loring S15 Falcon roaster and roasts five days a week, Monday to Friday.

The company's mission is "slow coffee for fast mornings": high-quality, traceable coffee served quickly. Nimbus became a certified B Corporation in 2021. The current CEO is Marco Albrecht; Lena Albrecht leads green coffee sourcing as Head of Coffee.
