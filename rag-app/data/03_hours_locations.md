# Hours and Locations

## Standard café hours
- Monday to Friday: 6:30 AM – 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: 7:30 AM – 4:00 PM

The Alder Street flagship in downtown Portland stays open later, until 8:00 PM every day.

## Holidays
All cafés are closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On New Year's Day cafés open at 9:00 AM and close at 3:00 PM.

## Locations
Nimbus has 8 cafés in Oregon (6 in Portland, 1 in Beaverton, 1 in Hillsboro) and 4 in Washington (2 in Vancouver, 2 in Seattle). The Hillsboro café is attached to the roastery and offers free public roastery tours every Saturday at 10:00 AM.
