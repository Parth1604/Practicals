# Sourcing and Sustainability

Nimbus buys green coffee through direct trade relationships whenever possible. In 2024, 78% of Nimbus's green coffee by volume was bought directly from producers or cooperatives.

Nimbus pays a minimum of $4.00 per pound (FOB) for all its coffee, well above the commodity market price. Lena Albrecht visits partner farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil at least once a year.

Sustainability commitments:
- All café cups are compostable, and customers with a reusable cup get $0.25 off any drink.
- Coffee chaff from the roastery is donated to local farms for compost.
- The Hillsboro roastery has run on 100% renewable electricity since 2022.
- 1% of annual revenue goes to the Nimbus Grower Fund, which finances water infrastructure projects in coffee-growing communities.
