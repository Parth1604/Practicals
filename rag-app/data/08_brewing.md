# Nimbus Brewing Guides

## Pour-over (V60)
Use 22 g of coffee to 350 g of water (about a 1:16 ratio). Grind medium-fine, similar to table salt. Water temperature: 94°C (201°F). Bloom with 50 g of water for 45 seconds, then pour slowly in circles. Total brew time should be 3:00–3:30.

## French press
Use 30 g of coarsely ground coffee to 500 g of water at 96°C. Steep for 4 minutes, break the crust, skim the foam, then press gently.

## Cold brew at home
Use a 1:8 ratio of coarse coffee to cold water (for example 100 g coffee to 800 g water). Steep in the fridge for 18 hours, then filter. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk before serving. Concentrate keeps for up to 10 days refrigerated.

## Storage tip
Store beans in an airtight container away from light. Nimbus recommends using beans within 4 weeks of the roast date and grinding just before brewing. Do not store beans in the fridge.
