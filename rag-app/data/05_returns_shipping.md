# Online Orders, Shipping, and Returns

## Shipping
Online orders of coffee beans and equipment ship from the Hillsboro roastery. Orders placed before 11:00 AM Pacific Time on a weekday ship the same day. Standard shipping costs $6.95 and takes 3–5 business days. Shipping is free on orders over $45 within the United States. Nimbus does not currently ship internationally.

## Freshness
Every bag is roasted no more than 48 hours before it ships, and the roast date is printed on the bag.

## Returns
Unopened coffee bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. If you don't like an opened bag, Nimbus offers a one-time "Happy Cup Guarantee": contact support within 14 days and they will send a different coffee of equal value for free. Brewing equipment can be returned unused within 60 days. Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise the customer pays return shipping.
